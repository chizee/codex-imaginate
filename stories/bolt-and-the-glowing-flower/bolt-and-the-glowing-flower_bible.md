# Character Bible

**Visual Style:** Soft, warm digital illustration style with rounded shapes and gentle textures. Colors are rich and inviting — warm copper tones, soft golden-pink glows, teal and blue accents. The junkyard is depicted as cozy and colorful rather than grimy. Characters have expressive, appealing designs with smooth lines. Lighting plays a key role, with the Glow Flower's light creating magical atmosphere. Style reminiscent of Pixar concept art meets children's picture book illustration. Slightly painterly with soft edges and visible warmth.

## Characters

### Bolt
- **Age:** Young (equivalent to a child)
- **Role:** protagonist
- **Appearance:** A small, round robot about the size of a toddler. Rusty copper-colored body with patches of teal paint. Big round blue LED eyes that glow softly. Two stubby arms with three-fingered claw hands. A little antenna on top of his head that wiggles when he's curious. He rolls on two small tank-like treads. Wears a tiny red scarf tied around his neck.
- **Personality:** Curious, gentle, optimistic, a little shy but brave when it matters
- **Notes:** Bolt's antenna glows brighter when he feels strong emotions. His eyes change shape to show expressions — circles for surprise, crescents for happiness.

### Pip
- **Age:** Young (equivalent to a child)
- **Role:** sidekick
- **Appearance:** A tiny mechanical bird, about the size of a sparrow. Made of shiny silver metal with golden wing tips. Has a small round head with bright green gem eyes. Her tail feathers are made of thin copper strips that jingle when she flies.
- **Personality:** Playful, chatty, loyal, a little mischievous
- **Notes:** Pip communicates in chirps and whistles. She lands on Bolt's antenna when she's excited.

### The Glow Flower
- **Age:** Newborn
- **Role:** symbol/catalyst
- **Appearance:** A small, delicate flower with five soft petals that glow with a warm golden-pink light. Its stem is a gentle green, and tiny sparkles float around it like fireflies. It grows from a crack between two old metal plates.
- **Personality:** Gentle, resilient, full of quiet life
- **Notes:** The flower pulses softly with light, almost like a heartbeat.

## Locations

### The Junkyard
A large, open-air junkyard filled with organized piles of old machines, gears, pipes, tires, and mechanical parts. Despite being a junkyard, it has a cozy, treasure-hunt atmosphere with warm sunlight filtering through gaps. Colors are warm rust, copper, teal, and silver. Paths wind between the piles like little streets.

### The Junkyard - Hidden Corner
A secluded nook between tall stacks of old tires and metal shelves. More shadowed than the rest of the junkyard, creating a private, secret feeling. The ground is packed earth with scattered small parts.

### Bolt's Workshop Corner
A cozy area Bolt has made his own, with a small workbench made from an old crate, organized piles of interesting finds, and a little sleeping nook. It feels like a child's bedroom made from found objects. Warm and personal.

### The Junkyard - Transformed
The same junkyard but now covered in glowing golden-pink flowers sprouting from every surface. Vines of light weave through old machines. The atmosphere is magical and alive, like a secret garden made of metal and light. Warm golden hour lighting.
