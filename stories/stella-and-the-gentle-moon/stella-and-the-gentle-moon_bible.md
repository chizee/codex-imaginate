# Character Bible

**Visual Style:** Soft, dreamy watercolor and digital illustration style with gentle gradients. Rounded, cute character designs with big expressive eyes. Rich indigo and deep blue night skies with warm golden and silver glowing elements. Soft light effects, sparkles, and translucent layers. Cozy, bedtime-story atmosphere with a palette of midnight blue, silver, warm gold, soft pink, and lavender.

## Characters

### Stella
- **Age:** Little star (young child equivalent)
- **Role:** protagonist
- **Appearance:** A tiny, soft-glowing star with a pale golden body, rosy pink cheeks, big round silver eyes, and five small rounded points. She wears a delicate ribbon of stardust around one point like a scarf. She is noticeably smaller than other stars.
- **Personality:** Shy, timid, gentle, curious, brave when encouraged
- **Notes:** Her glow flickers and dims when she is scared, and shines brightly when she feels safe and happy.

### Luna
- **Age:** Ancient and timeless (wise elder equivalent)
- **Role:** mentor/guardian
- **Appearance:** A large, serene crescent moon with a soft silver-white glow, gentle closed eyes with long lashes, a warm smile, and a crown of tiny twinkling stars along her top curve. Her surface has soft, pearlescent craters that look like freckles.
- **Personality:** Warm, patient, nurturing, wise, soothing
- **Notes:** Luna's light is steady and never flickers. She speaks in a calm, comforting way.

### The Other Stars
- **Age:** Various ages
- **Role:** side characters
- **Appearance:** Bright, confident stars of various sizes and colors — blue, white, and yellow. They have cheerful faces and twinkle boldly against the night sky.
- **Personality:** Friendly, playful, outgoing
- **Notes:** They mean well but don't understand why Stella is afraid.

## Locations

### The Twilight Sky
A transitional sky with warm orange and pink fading into deep blue and indigo. Wispy, soft purple-tinted clouds float gently. The horizon still holds the last glow of sunset.

### The Horizon Sky
The edge of the sky where a soft silver-white glow rises. The landscape below is silhouetted in dark blue — rolling hills and treetops. The sky above is deep indigo.

### The Moonlit Sky
A deep blue night sky bathed in soft, pearlescent silver moonlight. Gentle beams of light stretch across the sky. A few fluffy clouds catch the silver glow on their edges.

### The Starlit Sky
A rich, velvety indigo sky filled with colorful twinkling stars. Silver moonlight mixes with golden starlight creating a magical, sparkling atmosphere. The darkness feels soft and beautiful.

### The Night Sky
A wide, expansive view of the full night sky in deep midnight blue. Stars of all sizes twinkle peacefully. The overall feeling is vast but comforting, like a beautiful cosmic blanket.
