# Character Bible

**Visual Style:** Soft, dreamy watercolor-inspired digital illustrations with gentle pastel colors, fluffy textured clouds, and warm golden lighting. Characters have big, expressive eyes and rounded, friendly shapes. Sparkle and glow effects for magical moments. The overall feel is cozy, whimsical, and comforting — like a beloved picture book come to life.

## Characters

### Nimbus
- **Age:** Young cloud (child-like)
- **Role:** protagonist
- **Appearance:** A small, extra-fluffy white cloud with soft rounded edges, slightly smaller than the other clouds. Has big, expressive sky-blue eyes and a gentle, shy smile. A faint golden shimmer glows around his edges when he's happy. No rain falls from him — he stays perfectly dry underneath.
- **Personality:** Gentle, shy, kind-hearted, a little insecure but full of hidden joy and warmth
- **Notes:** Unlike other clouds, Nimbus cannot produce regular rain. His special rain only comes from laughter and joy, and it sparkles in rainbow colors.

### Cumulus
- **Age:** Older cloud (teen-like)
- **Role:** sidekick
- **Appearance:** A large, broad, friendly-looking gray cloud with a wide face, kind hazel eyes, and a reassuring smile. Wears a tiny lightning-bolt pin on his side. Has a slightly darker underbelly showing he's good at making rain.
- **Personality:** Encouraging, patient, warm, a good friend who sees the best in others
- **Notes:** Cumulus is the first cloud to notice Nimbus is sad and tries to help him fit in.

### The Flower Meadow
- **Age:** N/A
- **Role:** supporting
- **Appearance:** A vibrant meadow full of colorful wildflowers — daisies, poppies, sunflowers, and lavender — all drooping and thirsty, with a small brown bird sitting on a wilting sunflower.
- **Personality:** Hopeful, grateful
- **Notes:** The meadow and its creatures are the ones who help Nimbus discover his gift.

## Locations

### The Sky - Cloud School
A bright, open expanse of blue sky with fluffy white clouds arranged in a semicircle, like a classroom. Soft sunlight streams from the upper corner. The atmosphere is bright and cheerful.

### The Sky - Quiet Corner
A quieter, more remote area of sky at the edge, with cooler blue-purple tones. A few wispy clouds drift by. The mood is peaceful but lonely.

### The Flower Meadow
A vibrant patchwork meadow seen from above, filled with daisies, poppies, sunflowers, and lavender. Rich green grass between colorful blooms. A small wooden fence borders one side. Warm, natural sunlight.

### The Sky - Above the Meadow
Open sky directly above the meadow, bright blue with a few scattered wisps. A clear view down to the flowers below. The light is warm and golden.

### The Sky - Sunset
A breathtaking sunset sky painted in warm oranges, pinks, purples, and gold. Soft, glowing light bathes everything. A few distant clouds catch the colorful light.

### The Sky - Wide Open Sky
A vast, expansive sky stretching in all directions, bright and clear with a warm golden glow. Below, a beautiful patchwork landscape of blooming gardens, green forests, and winding rivers stretches to the horizon.
