# Character Bible

**Visual Style:** Soft, warm watercolor-style illustrations with gentle pastel tones and delicate hand-drawn outlines. Dreamy, storybook quality with slightly rounded shapes, cozy textures, and glowing light effects. Inspired by classic children's picture books like Beatrix Potter and Ernest Shepard, with a modern whimsical touch. Characters have expressive, cute faces with big eyes and soft features.

## Characters

### Barnaby
- **Age:** Young bunny (child)
- **Role:** protagonist
- **Appearance:** A small, fluffy cream-colored bunny with one floppy left ear, big round brown eyes, a tiny pink nose, rosy cheeks, and a puffy white tail. Wears a little blue scarf around his neck.
- **Personality:** Curious, gentle, brave, and full of wonder
- **Notes:** His left ear always flops down while the right one stands up straight

### Pip
- **Age:** Young bird (child)
- **Role:** sidekick
- **Appearance:** A tiny round robin with warm orange-red breast feathers, dark brown wings, bright black bead-like eyes, and a cheerful little yellow beak. Slightly smaller than Barnaby.
- **Personality:** Cheerful, talkative, loyal, and encouraging
- **Notes:** Flutters around Barnaby's head when excited

### Mama Bunny
- **Age:** Adult
- **Role:** supporting
- **Appearance:** A larger cream-colored bunny with both ears standing straight, soft brown eyes, and a warm smile. Wears a daisy tucked behind one ear.
- **Personality:** Loving, patient, and wise
- **Notes:** Barnaby's mother, appears in the final scene

## Locations

### Barnaby's Burrow Entrance
A cozy round doorway carved into a soft grassy hill, framed by tiny wildflowers and clover. The entrance has smooth, warm brown earth walls with visible root patterns. A small welcome mat of woven grass sits at the threshold.

### The Sparkling Meadow
A wide, lush green meadow dotted with colorful wildflowers — daisies, buttercups, poppies, and lavender. Gentle rolling hills in the background. After rain, everything glistens with water droplets under breaking sunlight.

### Wildflower Field
A dense, tall field of mixed wildflowers reaching high above bunny-height. Daisies, sunflowers, purple lupines, and golden buttercups create a vibrant, jungle-like canopy of color. Butterflies and pollen float in warm sunbeams.

### The Babbling Brook
A small, clear stream with smooth colorful pebbles on the bottom, bordered by lush green ferns and mossy rocks. A gnarled, moss-covered log stretches across it as a natural bridge. Dragonflies hover over the sparkling water.

### The Old Oak Tree
A magnificent, ancient oak tree with a wide trunk, gnarled roots spreading across the ground, and a vast canopy of green leaves. Soft moss covers its base. It stands alone in a small clearing with short, soft grass.

### Inside Barnaby's Burrow
A warm, round underground room with smooth earthen walls. Furnished with a tiny wooden bed with a patchwork quilt, a little shelf with acorn cups, hanging dried herbs and flowers from the ceiling, and small flower pots. Cozy and homey with soft ambient lighting.
