# Character Bible

**Visual Style:** Soft, whimsical digital illustration with gentle watercolor textures. Rounded, friendly shapes with warm pastel color palettes. Characters have large expressive eyes and soft edges. Lighting is always warm and glowing, like golden hour. Inspired by modern children's picture books with a cozy, storybook feel.

## Characters

### Barnaby
- **Age:** Young child (dragon years)
- **Role:** protagonist
- **Appearance:** A small, chubby dragon with soft mint-green scales, a pale yellow belly, big round amber eyes, tiny stubby wings that seem too small for his round body, little orange spikes along his back, and a short tail with a heart-shaped tip. He wears a tiny red scarf around his neck.
- **Personality:** Shy, gentle, a little scared of heights, but determined and kind-hearted
- **Notes:** He is the last dragon in his family to learn how to fly

### Pip
- **Age:** Young bird
- **Role:** sidekick
- **Appearance:** A tiny, fluffy sky-blue bird with a bright orange beak, big sparkly black eyes, rosy cheek patches, and a little tuft of feathers on top of his head that bounces when he moves. He has delicate white-tipped wings.
- **Personality:** Cheerful, encouraging, brave for his size, endlessly patient and optimistic
- **Notes:** Pip becomes Barnaby's flying coach and best friend

### Mama Dragon
- **Age:** Adult
- **Role:** supporting
- **Appearance:** A large, graceful dragon with deep emerald-green scales, warm golden eyes, long elegant wings, and a gentle smile. She wears a necklace of smooth river stones.
- **Personality:** Loving, patient, supportive
- **Notes:** She watches proudly from the cave entrance

## Locations

### The High Cliff
A tall, grassy cliff with wildflowers in yellow, purple, and white. The edge is soft and rounded with mossy patches. A stunning view of a lush valley below with a winding silver river. A cozy cave entrance sits behind the cliff top with warm light glowing from inside.

### The Practice Meadow
A gentle, sloping meadow near the cliff base with soft green grass, small mossy rocks of varying heights, shallow puddles, and stepping stones. Butterflies flutter among daisies. A safe, welcoming space for learning.

### The Windy Hilltop
A rounded hilltop covered in tall, swaying grass and dandelion plants. The wind is visible through floating seeds and bending grass. The sky feels very open and vast from this vantage point.

### The Open Sky
A brilliant blue sky filled with fluffy, cotton-like white clouds. Sunlight streams through gaps in the clouds creating golden rays. A faint rainbow arcs across the distance. The world below is a beautiful patchwork of green and gold.
